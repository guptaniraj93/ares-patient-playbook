System of record & integration plan

Clinical identity, diagnoses, consent, care plan: Practice Fusion. Operational enrollment and task status: CRM. Call and SMS history: Aircall. Video visits: Google Meet (interim, only after compliance approval; clinical note in EHR). AI meeting notes: Fireflies only after separately approved BAA, private storage, recording consent and retention safeguards; physician-verified note in EHR. Device readings: CCIQ. Qualifying minutes: CCIQ, subject to time/billing verification. Claims, payments and denials: Grove billing system.

Proposed integration sequence

  1. Aircall → CRM: call logs, lead transfers and outcomes.
  2. Practice Fusion → CRM: verified enrollment status; welcome SMS only after CCM enrollment and appropriate messaging consent.
  3. CCIQ → CRM: missed readings, device alerts and monthly task queues.
  4. CCIQ → Grove billing: reconcile qualifying minutes and RPM data evidence.
  5. Grove billing → CRM: claim readiness, denials and follow-up status.

These integrations are not confirmed live. Validate APIs, BAAs, access controls, and the event that determines successful onboarding.

Daily cycle

Morning: review and distribute tasks → Midday: scheduled CCM and outreach → Afternoon: retry and resolve → Close: reconcile, document and hand off unresolved tasks.

Every CRM task needs a patient reference, owner, priority, due time, reason, preference, attempt history, outcome and next action.

Patient & team call scripts

Draft scripts adapted to the Ares workflow. These are conversation guides, not word-for-word legal or clinical consent language. Grove's clinical, billing and compliance leads must approve scripts before operational use. No real patient information is stored here.

Interim telehealth compliance gate

Use Google Meet as the preferred video platform only on a covered, correctly configured Google Workspace account with an executed BAA. Fireflies.ai may be used only after confirming Enterprise HIPAA eligibility, Private Storage, an executed BAA, appropriate patient notice/consent, and an approved retention and access policy. Until these gates are completed, do not invite the Fireflies bot or use it to record, transcribe, upload, or process patient encounters. Use clinician-authored documentation in Practice Fusion instead.

Maintain separate vendor UX reviews with Doxy.me (elderly-friendly one-tap joining and phone-to-video transition) and Aircall (live transfer, SMS link delivery, failed-call recovery, CRM events); no vendor discussion has occurred yet. The physician remains responsible for reviewing and finalizing documentation in Practice Fusion, and the CRM remains responsible for follow-ups.

System-of-record rules

CRM owns task coordination; Practice Fusion owns clinical documentation. Initial contact is only the live transfer from the lead-transfer representative; do not schedule or send any pre-enrollment Ares initial-call SMS. Send a welcome SMS only after CCM enrollment is confirmed and consent requirements are met. Aircall provides voice and SMS, ChronicCareIQ provides connected-device data, and Google Meet hosts video visits. The patient experience remains phone-first, with Google Meet for interim video visits and Fireflies disabled for patient PHI until all HIPAA and consent prerequisites are verified. The Practice Fusion patient portal and ChronicCareIQ patient messaging are not used.
