Daily cycle

Morning: review and distribute tasks → Midday: scheduled CCM and outreach → Afternoon: retry and resolve → Close: reconcile, document and hand off unresolved tasks.

Every CRM task needs a patient reference, owner, priority, due time, reason, preference, attempt history, outcome and next action.

Aircall SMS Template Library

Generic administrative messages only. Confirm SMS consent, quiet hours, accuracy of timing and preferred contact channel before sending. The buttons copy text; they do not send SMS.

System-of-record rules

CRM owns task coordination; Practice Fusion owns clinical documentation. Aircall provides voice and SMS, ChronicCareIQ provides connected-device data, and Doxy.me hosts video visits. The patient experience remains phone-first. The Practice Fusion patient portal and ChronicCareIQ patient messaging are not used.

Integration and automation designs shown here are specifications, not connected services. Do not enter real patient data into this static site.

Open decisions and approvals
