System of record & integration plan

Clinical identity, diagnoses, consent, care plan: Practice Fusion. Operational enrollment and task status: CRM. Call and SMS history: Aircall. Video visits: Doxy.me (clinical note in EHR). Device readings: CCIQ. Qualifying minutes: CCIQ, subject to time/billing verification. Claims, payments and denials: Grove billing system.

Proposed integration sequence

  1. Aircall → CRM: call logs, lead transfers and outcomes.
  2. Practice Fusion → CRM: verified enrollment status; welcome SMS only after CCM enrollment and appropriate messaging consent.
  3. CCIQ → CRM: missed readings, device alerts and monthly task queues.
  4. CCIQ → Grove billing: reconcile qualifying minutes and RPM data evidence.
  5. Grove billing → CRM: claim readiness, denials and follow-up status.

These integrations are not confirmed live. Validate APIs, BAAs, access controls, and the event that determines successful onboarding.

Daily cycle

Morning: review and distribute tasks → Midday: scheduled CCM and outreach → Afternoon: retry and resolve → Close: reconcile, document and hand off unresolved tasks.

Every CRM task needs a patient reference, owner, priority, due time, reason, preference, attempt history, outcome and next action.

Aircall SMS Template Library

Generic administrative messages only. Confirm SMS consent, quiet hours, accuracy of timing and preferred contact channel before sending. No SMS is sent before initial live transfer; welcome SMS is sent only after confirmed CCM enrollment and appropriate SMS consent. The buttons copy text; they do not send SMS.

System-of-record rules

CRM owns task coordination; Practice Fusion owns clinical documentation. Initial contact is only the live transfer from the lead-transfer representative; do not schedule or send any pre-enrollment Ares initial-call SMS. Send a welcome SMS only after CCM enrollment is confirmed and consent requirements are met. Aircall provides voice and SMS, ChronicCareIQ provides connected-device data, and Doxy.me hosts video visits. The patient experience remains phone-first. The Practice Fusion patient portal and ChronicCareIQ patient messaging are not used.

Integration and automation designs shown here are specifications, not connected services. Do not enter real patient data into this static site.
